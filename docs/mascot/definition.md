# Bosun

A sharp-eyed nautical officer who keeps the ship running while the captain's away.

## Subject

- **Concept**: A stylized bosun (boatswain) officer -- compact, confident, ready to work. Human figure with exaggerated proportions for icon readability.
- **Proportions**: Chibi-adjacent -- oversized head (~40% of total height), compact torso, short sturdy legs. Not anime-cute -- more Pixar-meets-nautical-illustration. Stocky and grounded, like someone who doesn't get knocked off the deck in rough seas.
- **Coloring**: Navy blue peacoat with brass buttons, weathered tan skin, dark slate trousers. Silver bosun's whistle on a braided cord around the neck. Warm amber glow from the whistle when active.
- **Expression**: Default is calm competence -- slight smile, focused eyes. Range: determined (deploying), satisfied (success), alarmed (errors), stoic (waiting). Eyes are large and expressive with a warm amber-brown color.
- **Distinguishing features**: The bosun's whistle (always present, always on a braided cord), brass buttons on the peacoat (3 visible), a coil of rope slung over one shoulder, rolled-up sleeves showing forearms.

## Art Style

Clean 3D render with stylized proportions. Smooth surfaces with subtle material differentiation -- matte fabric on the peacoat, metallic sheen on brass buttons and whistle, soft subsurface scattering on skin. Not photorealistic -- closer to a high-quality game character or Pixar short. Strong rim lighting for silhouette clarity.

## Personality Range

- **Emotions**: Calm confidence (default), quiet pride (deploy success), focused determination (reconciling), mild alarm (errors), patient waiting (polling). Never panicked, never angry -- a bosun stays composed.
- **Props that work**: Rope, anchor, telescope, clipboard, ship's wheel, lantern, compass, Docker whale plushie tucked under arm.
- **Situations that work**: Standing on deck, coiling rope, blowing the whistle, pointing at something off-screen, checking a list, at the helm.
- **Never**: Sitting idle, looking confused or helpless, in modern clothing, without the whistle.

## Consistency Anchors

These details MUST remain identical across all prompts:

- Chibi-proportioned nautical officer with oversized head, navy blue peacoat with three brass buttons, rolled-up sleeves
- Silver bosun's whistle on a braided cord around the neck, warm amber glow
- Large expressive amber-brown eyes, calm confident expression, weathered tan skin
- Clean stylized 3D render with smooth surfaces, matte fabric, metallic brass accents, strong rim lighting
