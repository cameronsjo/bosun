# Subject

An isometric technical illustration of a GitOps pipeline rendered as a nautical scene. A stylized yacht (server) sits at center, with clean geometric compartments visible inside showing the pipeline stages as glowing stations connected by flowing lines of light:

1. A radio antenna receiving a signal from above (webhook)
2. A scroll unfurling (git clone)
3. A locked chest opening with a golden key (SOPS decrypt)
4. A workbench with blueprints transforming into finished documents (Go templates)
5. Cargo being loaded onto the deck (deploy)
6. Small crew figures (containers) standing at attention on deck

Above the yacht, a compass rose with a GitHub octocat silhouette at its center represents the Captain. A glowing amber line connects the compass to the radio antenna. Below the yacht, a magnifying glass sweeps across the deck (drift detection).

# Style

Clean stylized 3D render with an isometric perspective. Low-poly geometric forms with smooth shading. Glowing connection lines in cyan and amber. Each pipeline stage is a distinct, labeled station with subtle iconography. Technical illustration meets nautical charm -- like a cross-section diagram from a beautiful engineering manual.

# Lighting

Cool ambient fill from above. Warm amber glow from active pipeline stations. Cyan accent lighting on connection lines between stages. Each compartment has its own subtle interior glow. Strong rim lighting on the yacht's hull edges for silhouette separation.

# Environment

Plain solid black background. No ocean, no waves, no sky. The yacht and pipeline float in void. No floor reflections, no ground plane.

# Composition

Wide 16:9 frame. Yacht centered, filling approximately 70% of the frame. Isometric angle (roughly 30 degrees). Pipeline flows left-to-right through the yacht's compartments. Clean silhouette edges. Minimal negative space around the yacht.
